The founding subtraction.

The price of a coffee. Not the price of a cafe.

India fell in love with specialty coffee and never built a daily price point for it. We are building for the person who drinks it every day.

A cafe’s price carries its room: the seating, the air-conditioning, the address, the floor you occupy one chair of. We are building a format where the price only has to carry the cup.

We are not lowering the bean standard to get there. We are removing everything around the bean. That is the whole pricing story, and we would rather you noticed it on your second visit than read about it on your first.

The postscript.

Why P.S.?

A postscript is the thing you write after you think the letter is done. The thought that refused to stay quiet. The line that turns out to matter most.

A postscript needs a letter. The letter is your day: the meetings, the deadlines, the lectures, the commute. We are the line you add after it.

The day is the letter. The coffee is the P.S.

Plain & Simple.

No jargon, no origin lecture, just the cup. Specialty grade without the specialty theatre.

Mission.

To put a serious cup within ninety seconds of where people actually spend their day.

Not a destination. Not a treat. The coffee you have on an ordinary Tuesday, made to a standard that has no business being available on an ordinary Tuesday.

Vision.

A working day where good coffee is ordinary, and still worth looking forward to.

Making good coffee ordinary is one part. Keeping it worth looking forward to is the part we are building for.

We will never mix your milk with instant coffee.

Never instant.We will never use instant coffee powder in your cup.

Ground at order.We plan to grind for every order, never in bulk for the day.

Nothing hidden.We will tell you what is in the cup. The same standard will apply to the four on the board and the rotating drinks.
