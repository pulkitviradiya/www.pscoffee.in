The plan, in three steps.

Step 01

Choose your Pass.

Pick the pack that matches your habit. Each proposed Pass has a stated validity window and cup limit. Unused cups would expire at the end of that window. No automatic renewal is planned.

Step 02

Order on the app.

Planned for launch: choose your drink and pick-up time in the app. An eligible Pass would apply automatically.

Step 03

Show up. That is it.

The plan: coffee made fresh for your chosen pick-up time at a participating Pod.
