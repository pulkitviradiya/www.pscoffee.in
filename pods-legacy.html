What is a P.S. Pod?

A small space. A serious cup.

We are developing compact Pods for fresh coffee and grab-and-go service, inside a building or on a nearby corner.

The Letter is the familiar starting point. The P.S. is the range behind it.

The Pod format.

Size.A planned footprint of 50–200 sq ft, assessed for each site.

Service.Grab-and-go, with preparation and pick-up designed together.

Format.In your building, or on the corner outside it. Grab-and-go, with no seating.

Technology.Planned app pre-orders, with your choice of pick-up time.

Quality.Our standard: honest specialty coffee, ground at order.

Missing on purpose.Seating and table service. The focus is the cup, ready to take with you.

Why Pods?

Small on purpose. Inside your day.

Four drinks are planned for the board. A rotating handful will sit behind them. The Pod is being built around the everyday cup.
