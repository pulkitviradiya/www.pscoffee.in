What an office coffee Pod is.

A Pod is a compact coffee counter, 50 to 100 square feet, placed inside your building or on the corner outside it. There is no seating, no table service and no dining room to air-condition. What is left is a serious machine, specialty-grade beans and a person trained to use both. Walk up, pick up, go.

It fits where a vending bank or a photocopier currently sits. Most of the work of putting one in is deciding where it goes.

What the 9am and the 3pm actually need.

An office coffee habit is not one rush. It is a wave at nine, a slower one after lunch, and a third around four that decides how the last two hours go. The pantry machine serves whoever is nearest, the same way, all day. A cafe two streets away costs twenty minutes in each direction.

A Pod is built for the gap between those two options: the same cup as the specialty cafe, made properly, without anyone leaving the building.

What we bring, and what you provide.

  • We bring the counter, the equipment, the beans and the barista.
  • We handle training, supply, maintenance and the daily running of it.

It sits alongside what you already run.

A Pod is the coffee counter, not the canteen. It works alongside whatever cafeteria, catering contract or meal-card arrangement your building already has, and covers the one thing those setups rarely do well: specialty coffee, ground at order, in under a minute. If you already run a food vendor, nothing about that has to change.

Something new every few weeks.

Four drinks are always on the board: Black, White, Green and Strong. Behind them sits a rotating set of versions that changes every few weeks. That is the difference between an amenity people use and one they get bored of by March.

P.S. A cup your people have every working day is allowed to be interesting.
