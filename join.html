We are building for the daily drinker. If the cup, the range and the details matter to you, we would like to hear from you.

What we value.

Four things we look for in the people building P.S. Coffee.

01

Craft without ego.

You care about the quality of what you make. You do not care whether anyone notices. The cup is the proof.

02

Speed with care.

Grab-and-go speed. Specialty standards. You do not let one cancel the other.

03

Curiosity about the cup.

A familiar core, with a changing range behind it. If trying the next recipe sounds like the interesting part, we should talk.

04

Directness.

We say what we mean. We mean what we say. You will fit in here if you do the same.
