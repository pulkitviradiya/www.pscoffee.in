The founding subtraction.

The price of a coffee. Not the price of a cafe.

A Pod has no seating, no Wi-Fi, no dining room to cool. You do not use those things, so you do not pay for them. That is the whole pricing model.

Consider what a cafe's price is carrying. The room. The seating. The playlist. The air-conditioning across a floor you occupy one chair of. The table service. The address.

Now consider what you actually came for. We built a format where the price only has to carry the cup. We did not lower the bean standard to reach the price; we removed everything around the bean.

When the format costs less to build and run, the cup should cost less to buy. Passing that on is not a discount. It is just honesty. For years, the people who drink coffee every day have been quietly funding the experience of the people who visit once a week. We ended that arrangement.

P.S. We lowered our rent. Not our beans.

Founder note.

This began as an irritation, not a business plan.

The irritation was simple: serious daily drinkers were being asked to pay for a room they did not need.

P.S. Coffee is the attempt to make that contradiction disappear. Not by making coffee cheaper in spirit, but by making the format more honest. The cup should carry the cup, not the furniture around it.

We will never mix your milk with instant coffee.

Never instant.We will never use instant coffee powder in your cup.

Never stale.We will never use pre-ground coffee sitting in a hopper since morning.

Never hidden.We will never use Robusta beans hiding in a blend or substitute a cheaper ingredient to protect a margin.

Always the standard.100% Arabica single-origin beans, ground at every single order, pulled at the right temperature, with the right milk, at an honest price.
