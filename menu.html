ABOUT THIS MENU

Before you
choose.

Because a board you can read in three seconds is worth more at 9am than a wall of options. Black, White, Green and Strong cover the familiar starting points. The range sits behind them rather than on top of them.
They are the other ways to have the same drink. P.S. Black No. 2 is a different preparation of the same family, not a different product line. Numbers rather than invented names, because a number is sayable at a counter at 8:45am.
Not yet. Everything on this page is a planned item at a proposed price while we finalise sourcing and the first Pods. Prices will be confirmed before anything opens, and the Menu is where the numbers will live.
That is the plan, at no surcharge. Milk choice should not be a pricing decision. The recipe for each drink stays visible on its card so you can see what changes when you swap.
The protein family. Twenty grams of protein in the same coffee, without thinning the cup to make room for it. It is built for the gym stop rather than as a novelty on the board.
Yes, a small number of things worth grabbing alongside a cup. Not a restaurant and not a bakery. Availability will vary by Pod once they open.
