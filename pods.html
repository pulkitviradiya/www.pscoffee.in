What is a P.S. Pod?

Not a cafe. Not a kiosk. A precision instrument for one outcome.

A Pod is not a cafe. It is not a kiosk. It is a precision instrument for one outcome: your coffee, fresh, ready, in the building you are already in.

It does not ask you to sit down, wait around, or turn a daily cup into an occasion.

The Pod format.

Size.50-200 sq ft. Fits where a photocopier used to be.

Service.Grab-and-go. Under 90 seconds from order to cup.

Format.Inside your building. No seating. No detour. No queue culture.

Technology.Planned app pre-orders, with your choice of pick-up time.

Quality.Same 100% Arabica. Same fresh grind. Every Pod, every day.

Missing on purpose.Seating. Wi-Fi. Table service. Cutlery. A dining room to air-condition. You do not use them, so you do not pay for them.

Why Pods?

The Pod is small so the price can be honest.

We chose that deliberately. Everything a cafe adds around the cup costs money, and that cost has to live somewhere. In a cafe, it lives in your cup. In a Pod, it does not exist.
