## 1. Why this page exists

`/coffee-for-coworking-spaces` is the landing page: it answers "can you put a counter in our
space". This post is the lead magnet that gets an operator to that page — written for someone
running a co-working floor who has not yet decided that coffee is a problem worth solving.

It is a B2B post in the Journal, which is allowed and useful: the Journal is where a considered
argument can live without the landing page having to make it.

**Banned words that specifically threaten this page:** community, ecosystem, synergy, premium,
elevate, curated, world-class, member experience as a buzzword, any price or cost figure.

Note **community** in particular: it is banned brand vocabulary and it is the single most common
word in co-working marketing. A writer immersed in that category will reach for it repeatedly.

## 7. The risk on this page

`blog/why-coworking-spaces-ahmedabad-need-better-coffee.html` already exists and is local and
persuasive. **This post must be the operational one.** That post argues that Ahmedabad's
co-working coffee is bad; this one tells an operator what to do about it and what each option
costs them in work.

If a draft is mostly about how bad current co-working coffee is, it is the other post again.

Second risk: sounding like a sales page. The Journal's job here is to be useful enough that the
landing page is the obvious next click, not to do the landing page's job worse.

---

## 8. What this page must not do

- Name or imply a co-working brand, a coffee brand or a machine supplier
- Quote a price, a cost per cup or a commercial term
- Use "community", "ecosystem" or "synergy"
- Repeat the argument in `why-coworking-spaces-ahmedabad-need-better-coffee`
- Claim any Pod is open
- Carry more than one P.S. sign-off

---

## 9. How to deliver

**Format:** plain prose, any format you already use. Not HTML.

**What to hand over:** the H1 and lede; the body with H2s marked in order, with the four-setup
comparison as a simple table or clearly labelled list; the FAQ as five question-and-answer pairs,
40-60 words each, each written to stand alone; the single P.S. sign-off at the close.

**Word count:** 800-1,100 words, excluding the FAQ block.

**What not to try to write:** meta title, meta description, slug, kicker line, schema or
internal-link markup.
