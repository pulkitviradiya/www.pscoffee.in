## 1. Why this page exists

"Office coffee solution" and "vending machine alternative" are the two phrases a facilities
manager types when the complaints reach a threshold. Nobody writes an honest answer, because
everybody writing about it sells one of the two options.

This post is the one place the brand can compare formats at length. It is a **P2** in the plan for
a reason: it is the most likely of the eight to drift into something the brand does not publish.
Written well it is the strongest B2B asset on the site. Written carelessly it is an attack ad.

## 5. FAQ questions to include

40-60 words each, mirrored exactly into `FAQPage` JSON-LD regenerated from the visible HTML.

- What is the alternative to a vending machine for office coffee?
- Is an office coffee machine or a staffed counter better?
- Does an office coffee counter need a kitchen?
- What does a building have to provide for a coffee counter?
- When is a machine still the right answer?

That last question is the one that makes this post trustworthy. Answer it honestly and
specifically, or cut the post.

## 7. The risk on this page

**Competitor gravity, in its purest form.** The Handbook names this failure mode explicitly, and
this is the brief most exposed to it. The test is mechanical: if any sentence would embarrass the
brand were a machine supplier to read it aloud, cut it.

Second test, and the harder one: **would a facilities manager who just renewed a machine contract
finish this post without feeling got at?** If not, it is not publishable, however accurate it is.

---

## 8. What this page must not do

- Name or imply any machine supplier, coffee chain, vendor or platform
- Quote a price, a cost per cup, a saving or a percentage
- Mock the machine, the pantry, or the people who chose either
- Claim a Pod is open, or name a location
- Pretend the counter wins every case
- Carry more than one P.S. sign-off

---

## 9. How to deliver

**Format:** plain prose, any format you already use. Not HTML.

**What to hand over:** the H1 and lede; the body with H2s marked in order, with the comparison as
a simple table or clearly labelled list; the FAQ as five question-and-answer pairs, 40-60 words
each, each written to stand alone; the single P.S. sign-off at the close.

**Word count:** 800-1,100 words, excluding the FAQ block.

**What not to try to write:** meta title, meta description, slug, kicker line, schema or
internal-link markup.

**Read the "what it is actually for" section aloud before you send it.** If it sounds like a
setup rather than a fair case, the rest of the post will not survive review.
