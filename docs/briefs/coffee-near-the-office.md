## 1. Why this page exists

This is the consumer-side twin of `/coffee-for-offices`. That page sells to the building; this one
is written for the person standing in it at 8:55 with eleven minutes.

It targets the largest genuinely relevant consumer intent in the keyword set — someone searching
for coffee near work, before work, without a queue — and it is the natural top-of-funnel entry to
the whole workplace cluster.

## 7. The risk on this page

**Writing a listicle.** Every competing result for this query is "10 best cafes near X". If this
post becomes a list of places, it is both off-brand and instantly stale, and it would also mean
naming competitors, which is banned outright.

The post is an argument about time, not a directory. If a draft contains a numbered list of
venues, it has failed.

Second risk: **pretending we can solve it today.** We cannot. A reader who arrives, reads a
promise and finds no Pod is a worse outcome than one who reads an honest "not yet" and joins the
waitlist.

---

## 8. What this page must not do

- List, name or rank any cafe, chain or coffee brand
- Imply a P.S. Pod is open or orderable
- Moralise about mornings, discipline or productivity
- Argue price
- Use an exclamation mark anywhere
- Carry more than one P.S. sign-off
