The habit · 01

The ordinary moments.

A coffee can become part of the day through ordinary repetition. The stop before work. The pause between lectures. The cup after training. We are planning around those moments, without asking you to make an occasion of them.

The habit · 02

A cup worth returning for.

We want the coffee and the stop to work together. Honest specialty coffee matters. So does being in or around the places your day already runs through. A familiar order should have room around it.

The habit · 03

The postscript.

P.S. means Postscript. Also Pretty Serious. And Plain & Simple. It is the thought you add at the end, finding a place at the beginning of your day.

The habit · 04

The Letter and The P.S.

The Letter gives you a familiar starting point across Black, White, Green and Strong. The P.S. adds numbered variants behind those families. Return to a known recipe or try a different take. The opening slate is still being developed.
