Frequently asked questions

What does honest specialty coffee mean?+
It means describing the cup plainly and holding to a standard, rather than selling you an argument. The Menu says what goes into each drink. An Americano is espresso and water. You should be able to choose without reading a brand story first.
What is the difference between specialty and regular coffee?+
Specialty refers to a grading standard for the cup, based on how the coffee scores when it is tasted and how few defects the lot carries. Regular coffee carries no such standard. The preparation matters just as much once it reaches the counter.
Does the recipe change how the coffee tastes?+
Considerably. The same coffee prepared two ways gives you two different cups, which is the whole idea behind The P.S. The Letter gives you familiar starting points, and the recipe stays visible next to the family label so you know what you are choosing.
