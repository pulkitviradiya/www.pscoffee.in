What Is Cold Brew Coffee — And Why Is India Finally Ready for It?

By P.S. Coffee July 2026 5 min read

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for 12 to 24 hours instead of brewed hot. No heat, at any point in the process.

That single difference changes the chemistry of the cup: cold brew comes out naturally lower in acidity, smoother in texture, and cleaner in flavour than anything hot water can produce. It is not iced coffee. It is not instant cold coffee mixed with water. It is a genuinely different drink, and the difference is not marketing. It is chemistry.

Cold brew vs iced coffee — the actual difference.

Iced coffee is brewed hot, then chilled or poured over ice. The heat that pulled out its flavour also pulled out its acidity, so what you taste is sharper and shorter-lived. Left in the glass, it loses its edge within the hour.

Cold brew never sees heat. Coarse-ground beans steep in cold water for 12 or more hours, and that slow extraction pulls sweetness from the bean without pulling the harsh acids that hot water pulls. The result stays smooth, stays low-acid, and stays fresh for 5 to 7 days refrigerated.

For a country that spends the better part of eight months in some version of summer, that is not a trend. It is a format waiting for the season it was built for.

The short version: iced coffee is hot coffee that got cold. Cold brew was never hot at all.

Why Arabica makes better cold brew.

A 12-hour steep is not a gentle process. It draws out everything in the bean, the good and the bad. Run that process on Robusta and you amplify the bitterness Robusta already leans on. Run it on Arabica and you amplify the sweetness, the chocolate notes, and the clean finish Arabica already has.

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly is cold brew coffee?

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for 12 to 24 hours instead of being brewed hot. The slow, cold extraction produces a smoother, naturally sweeter, lower-acid cup than hot brewing methods.

Is cold brew the same as iced coffee?

No. Iced coffee is brewed hot and then chilled or poured over ice. Cold brew is never heated at any point, which is what gives it a different chemistry and flavour.

Why does cold brew taste less acidic?

Heat extracts acidic compounds quickly. Cold extraction over many hours pulls sweetness and body from the bean without pulling those same harsh acids.

Does cold brew have more caffeine than regular coffee?

Cold brew concentrate is often stronger by volume because of the long steep, though this depends on the ratio of coffee to water used. It stays less acidic regardless of caffeine strength.

Why does P.S. Coffee use Arabica for cold brew?

A 12-hour steep intensifies whatever is already in the bean. Arabica's natural sweetness and chocolate notes come through; Robusta's bitterness would only get sharper.

How long does cold brew stay fresh?

Refrigerated cold brew stays fresh for 5 to 7 days, considerably longer than iced coffee, which loses its flavour within hours of being made.
