Section 01 · What we are building

What P.S. Coffee is, in one paragraph

A grab-and-go specialty coffee brand. 100% Arabica. Freshly ground at every order. Pod format (50 to 200 square feet) embedded inside co-working spaces, corporate campuses, gyms, and university food courts. App-first ordering. Subscription model. Starting in Ahmedabad, then Surat and Vadodara. The website is live. The waitlist is growing. The Pods are coming. We are at the stage where the team that makes the first ones happen needs to exist.

The market context: India's specialty coffee moment is real, but the infrastructure for it is missing. The cafe model is expensive to operate, slow to scale, and locked out of the buildings where India's urban working generation actually spends its time. We are not building a cafe. We are building the beverage habit brand for the generation that works in a co-working space, not a coffee shop.

P.S. Coffee = Spot On Quality · Spot On Pricing · Spot On Reachability. The same 100% Arabica cup, inside the building you are already in, at ₹89. Not a compromise. Not a cafe. A Pod.

Section 02 · The roles

What we are looking for

We are building a small, tight founding team. Each role is genuinely founding-level: you will set the direction for your function, not execute someone else's plan. If you are looking for a defined job description with a reporting structure that already exists, this is not the right fit. If you are the kind of person who would write that job description, read on.

Role 01

Operations

You understand what running a QSR actually means at ground level: supply chain, vendor relationships, Pod setup, daily ops cadence. You have done something like this before and have strong opinions about how it should be done.

Role 02

Growth

You understand the audiences we are building for: co-working, gym, campus, corporate. You know how to build a waitlist, a community, and a daily habit. You think in audience, not in ads.

Role 03

Brand and Content

You write the way we think: direct, honest, witty without trying too hard. You would read this post and immediately know what to fix. You want the founding brief, not the content calendar someone else already wrote.

Role 04

Tech and Product

You think about UX the way we think about coffee: simple on the surface, considered underneath. You want to design the ordering app and Pod dashboard, not another SaaS feature nobody asked for.

We are not looking for someone who ticks every box. We are looking for someone who owns one thing deeply and cares about the whole brand. One co-founder who can hold the P.S. Coffee thesis across everything they touch is worth more than narrow specialists who do not understand what they are building.

Section 03 · What we offer

What we offer in return

We will be direct here, because the pitch does not need decoration.

An early equity stake. The terms depend on the role and the conversation, but founding-level contribution gets founding-level equity.

A brand with a clear thesis. P.S. Coffee is not an idea searching for a market. It is a defined format, a specific geography, a named customer, and a pricing position that works. You will not be spending the first six months figuring out what the company is. You will be building what it already knows it is.

A founding team that reads, thinks, and is honest about what it does not know. We are not going to tell you we have everything figured out. We have the thesis, the brand, the early traction signals, and the appetite to build. The rest is work, and that is what we are looking for people to do alongside us.

The first Pod in Ahmedabad. And, yes, the best coffee in the city once it opens.

This is not a startup born in a co-working space that has never run anything. The P.S. Coffee thesis is grounded in real market data: abCoffee's unit economics in India, Luckin Coffee's playbook in China, the specific consumption patterns of Gujarat's urban working generation. We know what we are building and why it works. What we need is the team to make it happen.

Frequently asked questions

What roles is P.S. Coffee looking for right now?+
We are building across Operations (QSR, supply chain, Pod readiness), Growth (co-working, campus, gym audiences), Brand and Content (writing, brand voice, founding brief), and Tech and Product (app, ordering system, Pod dashboard). These are founding-level positions, not staff roles. One person who understands the full thesis is worth more than narrow specialists who do not.
Do I need coffee industry experience to apply?+
No. Coffee knowledge is useful but not the point. What matters is deep expertise in your area (operations, growth, content, or tech) and a genuine interest in the P.S. Coffee thesis. We will teach coffee. You will teach us the domain you know better than we do.
Is this a full-time commitment or can I start part-time?+
We are open to both conversations. Founding-level equity requires founding-level commitment, and we will be direct about what that looks like. If you are currently employed and want to explore the conversation before making a decision, that is a reasonable place to start. Reach out and we will have an honest discussion about what the engagement looks like at different stages.
What does early equity mean in practice?+
A defined ownership stake in P.S. Coffee, earned through a founding role. The specifics (percentage, vesting schedule, cliff) depend on the role, the contribution, and the conversation. We are not going to publish numbers in a blog post. What we will say is that founding-level work gets founding-level reward, and we are not looking for a freelancer at a fixed fee.
How do I start a conversation?+
Use the Join Us page: it is the fastest route and it gives us the context we need for a useful first conversation. Or email hello@pscoffee.in directly. Tell us which role interests you, one thing you have built that is relevant, and why P.S. Coffee specifically. Short is fine. We read everything.
