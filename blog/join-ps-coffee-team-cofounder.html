The team comes first.

We are building the first P.S. Pods in Ahmedabad and looking for people across operations, growth, brand and product. The team comes before the first Pod. Tell us what you have built and what you would take ownership of.

The Pod · 02

The brief.

Honest specialty coffee, range across the menu and Pods that fit the daily route. The first city is Ahmedabad. There is practical work ahead in sourcing, service, product and locations.

The Pod · 03

The work ahead.

We are developing the operating plan alongside the menu and the first locations. We need people who can turn the brief into a working service. The website is collecting interest while we develop the first Pods.

The Pod · 04

Operations and the cup.

Help develop recipes, preparation, training and the day-to-day running of a Pod. The person making the cup and the systems supporting them both matter.

The Pod · 05

Brand, growth and product.

Help explain the range, assess locations and build the planned app experience. The Usual, the current slate and pick-up features are part of the product brief.

The Pod · 06

A conversation about the role.

Role, commitment and any equity terms will be discussed individually. Share your experience, the work you want to own and why this stage interests you.

The Pod · 07

Bring the intention.

The first cup is still ahead. Help us make it worth returning for. Use the Join page to tell us about your work.

P.S. The cup is the proof.

Frequently asked questions

Are there barista jobs in Ahmedabad?+
We are collecting interest rather than filling rostered shifts, because the first Pods are still being placed. Barista, operations, growth and product roles are all in scope. Tell us what you have built and what you would want to take ownership of.
Do I need specialty coffee experience to apply?+
Not necessarily. Preparation and training are being developed alongside the first menu, so intention and a willingness to learn the recipe count for as much as a CV. Operations, growth or product experience is useful in its own right.
What roles is P.S. Coffee hiring for?+
Operations, brand, growth and product, alongside the people who will actually make the cup. The work ahead covers sourcing, service, training, assessing locations and building the planned app. The team comes together before the first Pod opens.
