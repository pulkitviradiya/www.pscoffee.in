The habit · 02

The cup comes first.

We are developing the menu and the first Pods before making promises about the Pass. The existing drinks and proposed prices are available on the Menu.

The habit · 03

Terms will need to be clear.

Any eventual Pass will need its own confirmed pricing, inclusions, validity and redemption terms. Those details are not settled, so we are not publishing a ladder of plans.

The habit · 04

No opening date to announce.

P.S. Coffee is pre-launch. The first Pods are being planned for Ahmedabad. The Pass has no confirmed availability date.

The habit · 05

How to hear more.

Join the opening waitlist if you would like updates. You can also share what would matter to you in a Pass through the feedback form. There is no payment to make.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a P.S. Pass now?+
No. The Pass is still being developed and there is nothing to purchase. No plans, prices, validity or redemption terms are confirmed, so we are not publishing a ladder of options yet. Registering interest costs nothing and commits you to nothing.
How will a coffee subscription work?+
The shape is still being decided. Any eventual Pass will need confirmed pricing, inclusions, validity and redemption terms before it goes on sale, and none of those details are settled. The menu and the first Pods come first, in that order.
When will the P.S. Pass be available?+
There is no confirmed date. P.S. Coffee is pre-launch and the first Pods are still being planned for Ahmedabad. The opening waitlist is where Pass news will go once there is something real to announce, rather than a plan we are still arguing about internally.
