The details, in plain words.

Proposed terms & conditions.

Pre-launch preview. Pricing, eligibility and terms are subject to confirmation before sales open. Registering interest is not a purchase or reservation.

  • All prices are exclusive of GST. 5% GST is added at payment.
  • Packs are prepaid and non-refundable.
  • Validity runs from the date of purchase. Unused cups expire at the end of the validity window.
  • One redemption equals one eligible item. Size upgrades and milk swaps follow standard menu pricing.
  • Not redeemable on retail beans, merchandise, or packaged items.
  • Every Pack works at any P.S. Pod, not a single location.
  • Please read each Pack's inclusions and exclusions before purchase.
